Royal Challengers Bengaluru

Royal Challengers Bengaluru (RCB) is one of the most popular teams in the Indian Premier League. Known for its passionate fans and explosive batting lineup, RCB has consistently competed at a high level. Although the team has reached the IPL finals multiple times, they are still chasing their first championship title.

RCB Key Players

Virat Kohli (The Legend)

Role: Batter / Anchor

19th season with RCB; scored 657 runs in 2025; anchor and emotional leader of the squad.

Phil Salt ✈️ (The Explosive Opener)

Role: Wicket-Keeper / Batter

Aggressive opener; key to RCB title run; Powerplay destructor and primary wicket-keeper.

Devdutt Padikkal (The Prodigal Son)

Role: Batter

Provides left-handed stability in top order; excellent middle-over navigation alongside Kohli and Patidar.

Rajat Patidar (The Captain)

Role: Batter / Captain

Led RCB to IPL trophy; spin-demolisher at No. 4; calm leadership and clutch batting under pressure.

Venkatesh Iyer (The Star Signing)

Role: Batter / Medium Pace

Biggest 2026 auction buy; versatile left-hander in top 5; chips in with medium-pace wickets.

Tim David ✈️ (The Finisher)

Role: Batter

Powerhouse finisher; strike rate 180+ in 2025; bridges batting core and all-rounders.

Krunal Pandya (The All-Round Glue)

Role: Left-Arm Spinner / Batter

Player of the Match in 2025 Final; left-arm spin and smart batting provide balance; tactical brain in middle overs.

Romario Shepherd ✈️ (The X-Factor)

Role: Bowler / Lower Order Batter

Heavy-ball specialist; raw pace and lower-order power; reliable in crunch situations.

Bhuvneshwar Kumar (The Swing King)

Role: Fast Bowler

Veteran 2026 addition; leads new-ball attack; legendary swing and death-over intelligence.

Josh Hazlewood ✈️ (The Hoff)

Role: Fast Bowler

World’s consistent T20 pacer; 22 wickets in 2025; spearhead of RCB bowling unit.

Yash Dayal / Rasikh Dar (The Local Heroes)

Role: Bowler

Yash Dayal’s redemption story; all-phase bowler; Rasikh Dar steps in with deadly slower balls when needed.
