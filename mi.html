MUMBAI INDIANS

Mumbai Indians (MI) is one of the most successful teams in the Indian Premier League. The team is based in Mumbai and plays its home matches at the Wankhede Stadium. MI has won five IPL titles and is known for its strong team performance. Some of its star players include Hardik Pandya, Rohit Sharma, Jasprit Bumrah, and Suryakumar Yadav. The team is famous for its fighting spirit and strong bowling attack.

MI Key Players

Rohit Sharma (The Hitman)

Role: Captain / Batter

Led MI to 5 IPL titles; highest-ever run-scorer with 5,000+ runs. The most successful IPL captain in history.

Mitchell Santner

Role: All-Rounder

New Zealand all-rounder Mitchell Santner joined Mumbai Indians (MI) for ₹2 Crore in the 2026 IPL, bringing his tight left-arm spin and reliable lower-order finishing skills to the side

Suryakumar Yadav (Mr. 360)

Role: Batter

World’s #1 T20 batter; most consistent MI match-winner with strike rate 170+; most 50s in last five years.

Deepak Chahar

Role: bowler

He holds the record for the best-ever T20I bowling figures (6/7) and is a handy lower-order batsman for his teams.

Kieron Pollard (The Powerhouse)

Role: Batting All-Rounder

One-club man; 5 IPL titles, 250+ sixes; MI’s most feared finisher.

Hardik Pandya (The Dynamic All-Rounder)

Role: All-Rounder

4 IPL titles; explosive finisher and medium-pace bowler; home-grown superstar and current captain.

Tilak Varma

Role: batsman

No longer just a "youngster," he is now the reliable bridge between the top order and the finishers.

Quinton de Kock

Role: wicket-keeper-batter

The fan favorite is back! Re-signed for 2026 to reunite the iconic opening partnership with Rohit.

Lasith Malinga (Slinga Malinga)

Role: Fast Bowler

Highest wicket-taker in MI history (170); iconic final-ball IPL 2019 wicket; greatest IPL bowler ever.

Jasprit Bumrah (The Boom)

Role: Fast Bowler

World’s best death bowler; 5 IPL titles; economy under 7.5; MI’s global bowling spearhead.

Trent Boult (The Thunderbolt)

Role: Fast Bowler

King of the powerplay; 2020 title-winning season record for most wickets in first 6 overs; lethal opening duo with Bumrah.
