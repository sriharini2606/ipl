Lucknow Super Giants

Lucknow Super Giants (LSG) is one of the newest franchises in the Indian Premier League. The team made its debut in 2022 and quickly became competitive. Although LSG has not yet won an IPL title, they have consistently reached the playoffs in their early seasons.

LSG Key Players

Rishabh Pant (Captain)

Role: Captain / Wicketkeeper Batter

The most expensive player in IPL history (₹27 Crore). Leader of the new LSG era; destructive left-handed batter.

Nicholas Pooran (Vice-Captain)

Role: Wicketkeeper Batter

LSG’s most consistent overseas star; smashed 524 runs in 2025 season at strike rate 178.21. Team finisher.

Role: Fast Bowler

Mohammed Shami

Role: Fast Bowler

Purple Cap winner (2023); veteran experience with crucial powerplay wickets for LSG.
