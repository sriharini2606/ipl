Punjab Kings

Punjab Kings (PBKS) is a competitive team in the Indian Premier League. The franchise has been part of the IPL since 2008. Although the team has not won an IPL title yet, they reached the final in 2014 and continue striving for their first championship.

PBKS Key Players

Shreyas Iyer (The Captain & Anchor)

Role: Captain / Batter

Transformed PBKS; first captain to lead 3 teams to IPL finals; 604 runs in 2025, leading Punjab to their first final in over a decade.

Arshdeep Singh (The Local Legend)

Role: Fast Bowler

All-time leading wicket-taker; 2× T20 World Cup winner; defended 111 against KKR in 2025; first Indian to 100 T20I wickets.

Yuzvendra Chahal (The Wizard)

Role: Leg-Spin Bowler

IPL’s all-time leading wicket-taker; 4/28 to defend tiny total in 2025; first Indian with 6-wicket haul in T20Is.

Shashank Singh (The Ice-Cool Finisher)

Role: Batter

Elite finisher; averaged 50 with strike rate 150+ in 2024/25; unbeaten 61* to chase 200 against GT.

Marcus Stoinis (The Hulk)

Role: Batting All-Rounder

Premier overseas all-rounder; provides raw power in death overs; key in 2025 season to top the league standings.

Prabhsimran Singh (The Powerplay Aggressor)

Role: Batter

Homegrown talent; scored nearly 600 runs in 2025; provides high-octane starts demanded by Ricky Ponting’s coaching style.

Marco Jansen (The Left-Arm Threat)

Role: Fast Bowler

Tall South African; formed lethal duo with Arshdeep; nearly 40 wickets in 2025; swings ball at 140+ km/h in Powerplay.

Priyansh Arya (The Breakout Star)

Role: Batter

Third-highest run-getter in 2025 for PBKS; 545 runs at strike rate 182.27; future of Punjab batting.

Nehal Wadhera (The Middle-Order Engine)

Role: Batter

Versatile left-hander; pivotal in 2025 chases; ideal middle-order partner for Shreyas Iyer.

Harpreet Brar (The Spin Slasher)

Role: Spin All-Rounder

Ultimate utility player; big wicket-taker with low economy; holds middle-overs bowling together.

Lockie Ferguson (The Speed Merchant)

Role: Fast Bowler

X-factor; bowls 150+ km/h; used as enforcer in middle overs; adds raw pace PBKS lacked.
